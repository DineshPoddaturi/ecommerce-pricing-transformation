1. Understanding the tables:
    - olist_orders_dataset.csv; order_id, customer_id, order_status, order_purchase_timestamp, order_approved_at; Order-level activity, time series
    - olist_order_items_dataset.csv; order_id, product_id, seller_id, price, freight_value; Transaction-level pricing analysis
    - olist_products_dataset.csv; product_id, product_category_name, product_name_lenght, product_description_length, product_photos_qty; Product characteristics segmentation
    - olist_sellers_dataset.csv; seller_id, seller_city, seller_state; Seller-level analysis
    - olist_customers_dataset.csv; customer_id, customer_city, customer_state; Customer segmentation, geolocation
    - olist_order_reviews_dataset.csv; review_id, order_id, review_score; Customer satisfaction, quality-adjusted pricing
    - olist_geolocation_dataset.csv; geolocation_zip_code_prefix, geolocation_lat, geolocation_lng, geolocation_city, geolocation_state; Geographic segmentation, distance-based logistics
    - Other tables exist (olist_category_name_translation.csv for English names, olist_payments_dataset.csv) — useful for analytics, bonus insights.

2. Analytical Goals
    - Customer Segmentation & Pricing Elasticity
        - Segment Customers by
            - Geography (state/city)
            - Purchase frequency / repeat customers
            - Basket size (avg order value)
            - Product category preference
        - Estomate how price changes influence repeat purchases or order volume
        - Suggest targeted pricing strategies for each segment.
    - Seller/Marketplace Optimization
        - Identify sellers with:
            - High volume
            - High ratings
            - High cancellation or return rates
        - Simulate take-rate or commission impact per seller on GMV
        - Recommend optimal seller-level incentives.
    - Product-Level Recommendations
        - Which categories/products are price-sensitive?
        - Use historical transaction variance to estimate elasticity.
        - Propose bundling, discounting, or premium pricing strategies.
    - Demographic Analysis
        - Map high-volume zip codes or states.
        - Identify logistics bottlenecks or shipping cost sensitivities.
        - Suggest location-based pricing adjustments.
    - Board-Level Metric Simulation
        - GMV, revenue per category
        - Customer LTV per segment
        - Take-rate and margin impact
        - Optional: simulate scenarios (price increase by 5% in segment X, what happens to revenue)
3. Technical Approiach
    - Data Cleaning & Merging
        - Join orders → order items → products → customers → sellers.
        - Convert timestamps to datetime objects.
        - Handle missing values (freight_value, review_score, etc.).
    - Feature Engineering
        - Order-level:
            - Total spend, discounts, shipping impact
        - Customer-level:
            - Recency, frequency, monetary (RFM)
        - Seller-level:
            - Avg order value, cancellations, reviews
    - Modeling/Analysis
        - Price elasticity:
            - Regression: quantity ~ price + controls
            - Or use log-log models for elasticity estimation
        - Segmentation:
            - Clustering: K-means or hierarchical on RFM metrics
        - Scenario simulations:
            - Price increase/decrease
            - Shipping cost variation
            - Promotions
4. Deliverable/Presentation
    - Even as a portfolio project, think like a strategy consultant:
        - Executive summary: 1–2 pages (strategic recommendations, expected revenue impact)
        - Dashboard: Tableau / Power BI / Python (optional)
        - Notebook: Python or R
        - Use visualizations: top product categories, customer segments, elasticity curves
